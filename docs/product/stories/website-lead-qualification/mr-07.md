## Outcome

As a homeowner considering a project, I can complete an accessible, mobile-friendly qualification flow and receive a useful next step; Erik receives a complete, understandable brief in JobTread for both qualified and low-fit inquiries.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The flow collects project type, location, timeline, budget, description, photos, prior-building experience/referral source, and contact details, with progress, clear validation, and locally saved progress that the homeowner can clear.
2. Approved business rules classify fit consistently from project type, service area, budget, and timeline. The result includes a human-readable fit brief and explains the next step without exposing internal scoring details.
3. Low-fit homeowners receive Erik-approved, respectful alternatives and remain visible in JobTread with their submitted information and low-fit classification.
4. Qualified homeowners receive the fit brief and Erik-approved paid-design expectations before booking; the booking path is provided by MR-08.
5. Photos and applicable fields reach the intended JobTread records. Repeated submissions do not create unintended duplicates; delayed, partial, or failed delivery is detectable and recoverable without losing the lead.
6. The flow supports keyboard, screen-reader, mobile, and accessible error behavior. Sensitive data is handled only through approved boundaries and is not placed in client bundles, logs, or repository fixtures.
