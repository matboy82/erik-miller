# MR-10: Request reviews and publish only approved testimonials

**Status**: Approved
**Approval recorded**: Matt, 2026-10-02 (user: "all approved").
**Work item**: MR-10
**Epic**: [Website and lead qualification](../../epics/website-lead-qualification.md)
**Source**: WS-6 completed-job review flow
**Next phase**: Technical spec/work packages drafted; human approval required before implementation.

## Outcome

As Erik, I can request feedback after completed work and decide which customer reviews may appear on the website, while homeowners receive fair, non-incentivized requests.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Each completed eligible JobTread job triggers the approved customer survey and Google review request sequence with Erik-approved timing and copy.
2. The flow does not filter recipients based on expected review sentiment or offer incentives for reviews.
3. Submitted reviews remain unpublished on the website until Erik approves the exact content, attribution, and usage; unapproved, withdrawn, or edited items remain held.
4. Approved testimonials appear in the website's review layer with accurate attribution and a source link where permitted. Erik can pause or correct an item.
5. The operator can identify workflow failures and reconcile them without sending duplicate or unintended requests.
6. Customer contact details and private review data remain within approved service boundaries and are excluded from public assets and logs.

## Dependencies and constraints

- MR-06 provides the testimonial layer; MR-04 establishes JobTread integration boundaries. Verify JobTread workflow and review-provider capabilities before promising automated behavior.
- Erik approves trigger timing, survey/request copy, attribution/usage rights, and each website testimonial. Matt approves exact synthetic test operations separately.
- Reviews must follow applicable platform policies; no automatic publication or default timeout approval is permitted by this story.

## Out of scope

Review incentives, sentiment-based filtering, unrelated JobTread reconfiguration, paid reputation services, or publication without Erik's explicit item-level approval.

## Verification notes

Verify the completed-job trigger, safe test recipients, request count, pause/recovery behavior, no duplicate delivery, review hold/approval/edit/withdrawal transitions, and public content accuracy. Simulations do not establish live provider behavior.
