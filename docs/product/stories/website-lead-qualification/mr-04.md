No external account is provisioned as part of the recommendation.
6. The report lists verified constraints and remaining prerequisites for dependent stories. Unverified capabilities explicitly block the affected commitments; they are not treated as working. API keys, customer PII, raw sensitive responses, and private customer photos are absent from repository, fixtures, logs, and report evidence.
