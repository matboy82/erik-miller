# MR-08: Book and manage a qualified consultation

**Status**: Approved
**Approval recorded**: Matt, 2026-10-02 (user: "all approved").
**Work item**: MR-08
**Epic**: [Website and lead qualification](../../epics/website-lead-qualification.md)
**Source**: WS-4; current booking direction in accepted ADR 0007
**Next phase**: Technical spec/work packages drafted; human approval required before implementation.

## Outcome

As a qualified homeowner, I can book an available design consultation and manage it; Erik sees the appointment on his calendar and associated with the intended JobTread customer/job.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Qualified homeowners can see the approved design fee/package, appointment mode (phone or in-person), timezone-correct availability, and buffers before confirming a slot on Erik's Google Workspace Appointment Schedule page.
2. The schedule respects Erik's configured calendar conflicts, availability and buffers and sends the configured booking confirmation/reminders. Google Meet is not used.
3. Homeowners can cancel or reschedule through the Google booking page; Erik can change schedule, availability, phone/in-person mode, and copy in Google Calendar.
4. The appointment is associated with the intended homeowner and JobTread records, and Erik can verify that association.
5. If booking service access or required capabilities are unavailable, the approved test site presents a clear registered placeholder and does not claim that booking works. Production remains blocked until required capabilities pass QA.
6. Booking behavior is accessible on mobile and keyboard, and customer data and credentials remain outside public assets and logs.

## Dependencies and constraints

- MR-07 supplies the qualified path; accepted ADR 0007 selects Google Workspace Appointment Schedules, with phone/in-person modes and no video conference. The previously selected Calendly direction is superseded.
- Verify Erik's account entitlements, availability, timezone/conflict behavior, confirmation/reminder, cancellation/reschedule, and supported JobTread appointment association before production. Erik manages the booking page and provides availability, buffers, fee/package, and copy.
- No account provisioning, paid-plan selection, or external mutation is authorized by this story. Exact synthetic test operations require a separate approval.

## Out of scope

Changing the booking provider direction, native JobTread booking, sales follow-up automation, production launch, or unsupported booking capabilities.

## Verification notes

Use controlled synthetic users and non-deliverable contacts. Verify availability boundaries, concurrent booking conflict prevention, timezone/DST handling, reminders, cancellation/reschedule, and JobTread association; label docs-only or simulated evidence accurately.
