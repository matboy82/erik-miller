## Outcome

As Erik and Matt, we can turn approved completed work into a consistent set of useful portfolio and social content without creating an unsustainable per-project workload.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A documented workflow connects an approved portfolio entry to a Google Business Profile post and Facebook/Instagram copy, with templates by project type.
2. The workflow includes a quarterly case-study format for locally relevant service content, with factual/provenance and approval checks.
3. Ownership and cadence state what Erik supplies, what BIS prepares, who approves, and what remains manual; no public post occurs without the required owner approval.
4. One end-to-end pass uses a real completed job only after media/claim approvals and explicit authorization for each external publication.
5. The documented per-job process takes Erik under 30 minutes, measured in a walkthrough and recording excluded waiting time separately.

## Dependencies and constraints

- MR-09 supplies the approved portfolio entry; MR-06/MR-11 supply site and search conventions. Real content and Erik's approval are mandatory for the live pass.
- Matt approves the process/template set. Google Business Profile and social account access and platform requirements are verified before any posting; this story does not authorize external posting.
- Treat the epic's WS-7 bonus scope as included; do not add paid promotion, full SEO services, or autonomous posting.

## Out of scope

Paid ads, video creation, automatic publishing, ongoing SEO services, and unapproved public account changes.

## Verification notes

Review templates for accuracy and platform fit, run the timed workflow, retain the approved real-job provenance and authorized publication evidence, and distinguish prepared drafts from published posts.
