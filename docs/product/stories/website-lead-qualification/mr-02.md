## Outcome

As Erik, I can compare the three proposed visual directions and their words on the test site, then make an informed brand choice.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The test site starts with Direction A and provides an unmistakable review toolbar with A/B/C theme selection and copy selection. All three use the corresponding approved exploration palettes/type pairings and draft tagline, hero, and process-copy sets from the branding kit.
2. Changing the theme updates the page's full direction rather than only its accent color. Changing copy updates the applicable displayed words. The chosen theme survives reload, and a missing/invalid stored theme falls back to A.
3. A reviewer can operate the controls using keyboard or touch, see their current selections, and use the page on a mobile viewport without clipped controls or horizontal overflow. Focus, labels, and contrast meet WCAG 2.1 AA for this experience.
4. Review copy remains visibly Draft where approval or factual evidence is missing. The interface does not imply that A, a tagline, a logo, or a business claim is finally approved by Erik.
5. A production build requires a recorded brand lock tied to Erik's choice and its ADR. Its artifacts contain exactly one selected direction/copy set and no review-toolbar markup, switching/persistence code, or alternative tokens. A missing or invalid lock prevents the production build.
6. Controlled verification builds demonstrate isolation for A, B, and C without creating an actual Erik approval or altering the real brand lock.

## Dependencies and constraints

Use the approved [branding kit](../../../brand/branding-kit-2026-10-01.md) and existing review scaffold. Local review can proceed before MR-01; persistent remote review depends on MR-01. Erik's final choice is required for an actual production build, not for comparing draft directions.

## Out of scope

Choosing the final brand/tagline, designing the final logo, inventing trust claims, building the full service site, production deployment, and DNS cutover.

## Verification notes

QA should cover all theme/copy choices, reload and invalid saved state, keyboard/mobile use, and inspection of all-direction production verification artifacts. Existing `npm run check:production` evidence must be assessed against the full isolation criteria; a screenshot alone does not prove artifact isolation.
