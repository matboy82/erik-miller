# MR-05: Submit general inquiries to JobTread reliably

## Outcome

As a Treasure Valley homeowner with a general remodeling question, I can send my contact details and message through the website and receive clear confirmation; Erik can find the inquiry in JobTread, including when delivery is delayed or fails.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The contact path explains what information is requested and confirms successful receipt without exposing internal integration details.
2. A valid inquiry creates or updates the intended JobTread customer and records the inquiry as a general inquiry with the submitted, approved fields. Erik can identify and act on it in JobTread.
3. Invalid or incomplete submissions receive clear, accessible field-level guidance and are not sent to JobTread.
4. Repeated submission of the same inquiry does not create unintended duplicate customer or inquiry records; the behavior and limits are observable to the operator.
5. If JobTread is unavailable or accepts only part of an inquiry, the submission is retained safely for recovery, and an operator can identify delayed or failed delivery and reconcile it without losing or blindly duplicating the inquiry.
6. Contact data and photos, if accepted by the approved contract, are transmitted and retained only through approved server-side boundaries. Secrets and lead PII do not appear in client assets, repository fixtures, or application logs.
