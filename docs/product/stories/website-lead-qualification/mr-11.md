## Outcome

As a homeowner or search assistant looking for a Treasure Valley remodeler, I can find accurate, consistent information about Miller Remodeling and its services.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Eligible production pages have accurate business, service, review, and FAQ structured data as applicable; markup matches visible approved content and passes the agreed validators.
2. The production site has a valid sitemap, canonical URLs, robots rules, redirects from the approved old-URL map, and consistent business/profile links. Test builds remain noindex.
3. Assistant-readable business information and service coverage are accurate, sourced, and consistent with approved site content and business profiles.
4. A dated baseline records agreed branded and local search queries and assistant-citation observations, with source, method, and limitations; quarterly rechecks are defined without promising rankings or citations.
5. Analytics or measurement behavior follows an approved privacy/data contract and does not disclose lead PII. Production measurement is disabled until its separate readiness gate passes.

## Dependencies and constraints

- MR-06 supplies approved pages and business copy; MR-09/MR-10 supply eligible portfolio and review content. The production brand/content/placeholder gates remain mandatory.
- Verify current business/profile facts, old URLs, ownership, and measurement/privacy decisions before production. Matt owns analytics/privacy decisions; Erik validates business facts.
- No production SEO configuration, analytics deployment, profile posting, or DNS change is authorized here.

## Out of scope

Ranking guarantees, ongoing SEO retainer, paid ads, DNS cutover, unverified claims, and automatic content posting.

## Verification notes

Validate generated metadata and structured data against rendered pages, sitemap/canonicals/robots/redirects per environment, profile consistency, noindex preview behavior, and privacy-safe measurement. Preserve baseline evidence and method so later comparisons are reproducible.
