## Outcome

As a Treasure Valley homeowner considering a remodel, I can understand Miller Remodeling's services, process, service area, and verified project proof, then choose a useful next step.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The site provides home, kitchen, bathroom, whole-home, additions, ADU/mother-in-law, home-repair, about/process, portfolio-index, and contact pages, plus service-area pages for Eagle, Star, Meridian, Boise, Middleton, and Kuna.
2. Pages explain the paid design phase and five-step process in plain language, with clear paths to contact or begin qualification.
3. Verified project or review proof appears near relevant calls to action. Draft or substitute content is visibly identified and never presented as Miller work, a real endorsement, or a verified result.
4. Navigation and page content work at mobile and desktop sizes, support keyboard and screen-reader use, and meet the approved WCAG 2.1 AA verification plan.
5. Pages preserve the approved brand/content separation and performance quality bars. Production contains only the one Erik-approved brand direction and eligible, approved factual content.
