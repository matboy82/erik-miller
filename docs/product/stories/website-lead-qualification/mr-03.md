# MR-03: Review development content and replace it easily

## Outcome

As Matt and Erik, we can review realistic page content while real project material is being collected, then replace the temporary photos and writeups without rebuilding the layouts.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The development review includes a representative project photo and readable Draft writeup rather than lorem ipsum. Licensed online stock images are visibly labeled as development placeholders; the page does not present them as Miller work or attach invented customer endorsements/results.
2. Each substitute has an entry linked from `PLACEHOLDERS.md` recording its source, license/use permission, replacement owner, and unblock condition. New substitutes are registered when added; account or asset availability alone does not close an entry.
3. Photos, alt text, and writeup text can be replaced through their content records without edits to page layouts or components. No CMS product is selected by this story.
4. A replacement rehearsal uses a differently proportioned representative image and a longer writeup. The page remains readable on mobile, avoids distortion/overflow, and preserves meaningful alt text and image dimensions/loading behavior. If no approved Miller assets exist yet, the rehearsal uses labeled licensed substitutes and leaves the production placeholder open.
5. Replacing development content with approved Miller assets records provenance and approval, removes the corresponding visible Draft/stock labels, and closes only the matching placeholder after verification.
6. The production readiness/build path rejects included unresolved stock/Draft project content. Accepted production artifacts contain no development stock photos or invented writeups. The root rule that every Open placeholder blocks production remains in force.

## Dependencies and constraints

Matt has authorized stock assets and draft writeups for development/test use. Erik is collecting real photos; their absence does not block development review. MR-02 supplies the review surface; MR-01 is needed only for remote review. Production requires approved real Miller content. Keep the review site's mobile performance and accessibility quality bars while adding media.

## Out of scope

Full portfolio CMS/publishing workflow, all service-page copy, video production, stock assets in production, fake factual claims, and release authorization.

## Verification notes

QA should inspect visible labels and source/license records, rehearse content-only replacement, check mobile/image accessibility behavior, and verify failure for unresolved development content. Check that unused development assets are also excluded from production output rather than merely hidden in the UI.
