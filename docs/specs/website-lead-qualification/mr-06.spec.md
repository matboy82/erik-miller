Use shared Astro layouts/navigation and typed Content Layer records for reusable service-area/project facts. Preserve review-only controls and Draft notices in preview; production uses the accepted brand lock and eligible content checks. Copy must come from Erik-approved source material. Draft placeholder prose cannot assert prices, credentials, results, review counts or service coverage beyond approved facts. Keep all routes statically rendered unless an approved contract requires otherwise.
