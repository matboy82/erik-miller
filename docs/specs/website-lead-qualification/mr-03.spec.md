## Content and replacement contract

Create a local `projects` collection in `apps/web/src/content.config.ts`, backed by JSON records under `apps/web/src/content/projects/` and local raster images under `apps/web/src/assets/projects/`. Keep one stable record ID for the representative section; the page queries that record and passes validated data to a static Astro component. No hydration or new browser storage is needed.

The validated record owns:

| Field group | Required contract |
|---|---|
| Display | Stable ID, nonempty title, readable plain-text paragraphs, and meaningful nonempty image alt text. Escape prose as text; do not accept executable HTML. |
| Image | Local asset reference, positive intrinsic width/height matching the file, and SHA-256 identity. Reject remote runtime URLs, missing files, unsupported formats, traversal, and symlinks outside the content asset boundary. |
| Provenance | `origin: stock | miller`, `status: draft | approved`, source/creator, license or permission evidence, replacement owner, and a substitute/placeholder ID with its repository evidence path. Stock must remain Draft regardless of a purported approval. |
| Approval | For approved Miller content: Erik's identity, approval date, and repository sign-off evidence identifying the exact image hash and a revision/hash covering title, alt text, and all writeup paragraphs. Missing, inconsistent, or stale approval fails eligibility. |

Use the collection schema and one cohesive build-time content owner for validation, provenance checks, and eligibility. The component consumes display data and derived label state; it does not decide whether a record is approved. Build scripts/configuration consume the same eligibility contract. Do not add parallel schemas, a general content service, or a remote loader.

Add a substitute record under `docs/content/substitutes/<id>.md` for each temporary photo or writeup, linked from `PLACEHOLDERS.md`. Record exact source URL, creator, retrieval date, license/permission and retained evidence, asset hash or copy revision, owner, unblock condition, and Open/Closed status. A photo and writeup may share a record only when both are explicitly inventoried and independently accounted for. Adding a second substitute adds its own entry and preserves earlier history. Link them from CONTENT-01; that aggregate entry stays Open until every covered substitute is resolved.

Implementation obtains at least two differently proportioned licensed online photos, verifies permission for local development/test display, and retains source/license evidence before use. Do not hotlink. If rights cannot be established, use another licensed source; asset availability is insufficient evidence. Strip location/private metadata, avoid identifiable customers or private information, and retain required attribution visibly when the license requires it. Public evidence must contain no secrets or private customer material.

For replacement, edit the record and add its replacement asset; layouts/components stay unchanged. Licensed rehearsal substitutes remain `stock/draft`. Real Miller replacement records retain the former substitute history and add provenance and Erik's sign-off for image and copy. Derive removal of project Draft/stock labels only from validated approval. Verify rendering and matching evidence before closing only that substitute's entry. An edited image, alt text, or copy invalidates prior approval until the changed content is approved again. Unrelated root entries remain unchanged.

## Rendered behavior

Place the representative section below the hero, with a heading, image, and paragraphs. Stock displays a visible notice such as “Development stock photo — not Miller Remodeling work”; Draft prose displays “Draft example writeup.” Draft Miller material also stays visibly Draft. Write about an illustrative design scope without invented names, addresses, prices, testimonials, delivery results, or claims that Miller completed the pictured work.

Use local Astro image processing for responsive variants with explicit intrinsic dimensions, responsive width, preserved natural aspect ratio, and meaningful alt text from the record. Use lazy loading for this below-fold image and avoid promotion to a hero/LCP image. Longer paragraphs wrap without line clamping or truncation. Maintain contrast, semantic headings, keyboard navigation, and no horizontal overflow at 320px, 390px, and desktop at 200% zoom across A/B/C. Labels may not disappear through CSS or theme selection. License-required credit survives approval independently of Draft labels.

## Production readiness and artifact contract

Accepted 0004 records this durable content-lifecycle and publication policy. Every production web entry, including the root production command and workspace build with `SITE_BUILD=production`, runs canonical content/root-placeholder validation alongside the existing brand-lock validation before rendering. Reject any Open root placeholder, malformed/unknown placeholder status, unreadable register/evidence, included stock or Draft project record, absent required representative record, and unsupported or inconsistent approval. Emit the offending ID and reason without private content. Failure clears the fixed web output target so a stale candidate cannot look successful. Review builds remain usable with Open entries and labeled substitutes.

Select production content and imports before rendering. Keep development media outside `public/`, which Astro copies wholesale. Review-only provenance, stock references, Draft example prose, and unused media must not enter production bundles or manifests. Do not hide a substitute at runtime to pass readiness.

Extend the existing `verifyProduction(directory, direction, selection)` boundary without dropping its brand/font checks. Replace its current blanket binary rejection with an explicit allowlist of approved selected image derivatives, tied to source identity and build-generated output identities. The verifier inventories the entire output tree, rejects unknown/unreferenced binaries, symlinks, development originals/derivatives and embedded media payloads, and checks that the selected project text/alt/dimensions render. Inspect textual HTML/CSS/JS/data/maps as well as media bytes. A suffix or missing “Draft” string alone does not establish ownership. Unknown files fail closed. Build inventory is evidence checked against selected eligible inputs, not arbitrary permission to ship any file listed by the build.

Extend MR-02's disposable fixture boundary to include the new collection, content owner, assets, evidence, and register. Production-isolation fixtures replace Open entries and content only inside their isolated copy, with explicitly synthetic approval/provenance evidence and nonprivate geometric image fixtures; they must never label a stock photo as Miller work. Use the same validators and artifact contract. Synthetic evidence is supported only by fixture-local wiring, as with the existing brand fixtures, and is rejected by canonical production. No environment flag, alternative register path, or ordinary command may confer fixture approval. Preserve the real register, records, brand lock, and ADRs; clean fixtures on success/failure. Existing A/B/C and mixed-copy checks must continue passing under synthetic eligibility, while the real production path remains blocked.

## Verification and quality bars

Use approved Node `node:test` command/output seams, `verifyProduction`, and manual rendered-page inspection. No browser framework or public testing interface is added. Implement behavior in vertical red-to-green cycles; prove production exclusion/readiness sensitivity with restored mutations or equivalent negative controls.

| QA / story | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| C1 / AC1–2 | Visible photo and Draft prose, explicit stock identity, source/license/owner/unblock records linked from the root, and a second substitute registration preserving the first. |
| C2, B2 / AC3–5 | Record-only replacement diff with a differently proportioned licensed photo and longer writeup; A/B/C mobile/zoom screenshots, alt/dimension/loading and accessibility checks. | C3 / AC6 | Nonzero production results for stock/Draft records, any Open root entry, malformed/missing evidence, stale approval, missing record, and fixture/mode/path bypass attempts. Otherwise eligible synthetic builds inventory selected images/copy and exclude unused originals, derivatives, hidden copy, and embedded stock bytes. Show failure cleanup and unchanged real approval/register hashes. |

Pin the QA plan's **Required** references: branding-kit edition 2026-10-01, WCAG 2.1 AA, tested revision/dirty diff, `package-lock.json`, and `lighthouserc.cjs`. After adding media, run three mobile measurements for A/B/C with normal content and again for A/B/C with the replacement-media/long-copy state. Extend the existing disposable review-fixture/LHCI runner rather than introducing an application variant parameter. Record content and image hashes, visibly selected state, effective viewport/throttling, Chrome/Lighthouse/Node/npm versions, OS, and isolated report directories.

Preserve optimistic aggregation: best score per required category and minimum LCP across the three runs. Performance, accessibility, and best practices each require ≥90; LCP must be strictly <2500ms using the existing strict report checker. Retain individual reports and assertions; SEO stays warning-only for noindex. Manual applicable AA checks include alt text, contrast, headings, keyboard focus and reflow; automated scores do not establish conformance. Missing Chrome, incorrect settings, missing variation evidence, or screenshots without output inventories makes the affected result Unverified. Local results do not prove remote publication.

Run targeted content/guard Node tests, then `npm run check`, then the six-state mobile protocol. No product checks are claimed by this planning document. Approved real Miller content is an execution prerequisite for its C2 branch, not a reason to block the licensed rehearsal or falsify its result.
