# Miller mountain logo concept

The logo evolves the mountain badge on https://millerremodelingidaho.com/ into the new site's forest green, warm cream, and muted brass palette. It is a concept for Erik's review.

Assets:

- `docs/brand/concepts/miller-logo-mountain-concept.png`: transparent logo generated with the built-in image generation tool.
- `docs/brand/concepts/favicon-mountain-concept.svg`: scalable companion emblem, drawn in SVG for small browser tabs.
- `docs/brand/concepts/favicon-mountain-concept-32.png`: 32-pixel PNG favicon.
- `docs/brand/concepts/apple-touch-icon-mountain-concept.png`: 180-pixel touch icon.

Matt selected this logo on 2026-10-02 and requested its application to the site. The header uses the forest green and brass logo; the footer uses a monochrome light version for contrast. The matching SVG favicon, PNG fallback, and touch icon are installed. The original concept files remain here as source assets.

## Logo generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished logo concept for Miller Remodeling, an established home remodeling company in Idaho, matching a refined editorial website. Original identity reference (visually inspected): a circular mountain badge with a black ribbon reading MILLER in orange and Remodeling curved beneath. Evolve its mountain identity into a restrained, exceptionally clean mark. Asset: a horizontal logo lockup on a genuinely transparent background, generous clear space, no presentation board or mockup. Left: a compact circular emblem with a simple two-peak mountain silhouette subtly forming an M; solid deep forest green #1B2420 shapes with a restrained muted brass #A8824A circular outline, very few broad shapes, no mountain illustration detail. Right: exact text 'Miller' on the first line in a beautifully crafted warm editorial serif akin to Fraunces, and exact text 'REMODELING' below in smaller generously tracked uppercase sans serif, both forest green #1B2420. Strong professional typography, optical balance, flat vector-like edges, elegant and approachable, timeless local craftsmanship. Designed to sit on warm cream #F6F1E7 but background must remain fully transparent. Avoid gradients, shadows, paper texture, fine scenery, tools, orange ribbons, stock roof clipart, tagline, additional words, watermark. Only one logo, no variations. Make the emblem simple enough to adapt as a favicon.
