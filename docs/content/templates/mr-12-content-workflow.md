# MR-12 content workflow and templates

**Status**: Draft operating template
**Owner**: Erik supplies project facts and approves all claims; Matt / BIS maintains the workflow and prepares drafts

This is a manual workflow. It does not post to Google Business Profile, Facebook, or Instagram. Start only from an approved MR-09 portfolio entry and its exact source media.

## Workflow

1. Erik selects one completed project and provides its approved portfolio entry, project type/location, publishable photos, and any review link with usage permission.
2. BIS copies only source-backed facts into the templates below. Each claim links to the portfolio entry or another approved source.
3. Erik reviews every claim, caption, crop, alt description, link, and call to action. Record the exact approved revision and date.
4. The account owner checks current platform requirements, pastes the approved content, and separately authorizes each post.
5. Record the published URL and date. Keep prepared, approved, and published states distinct. Correct or remove a post through the owning account if a source changes.

Do not include customer names, addresses, private messages, identifying home details, unapproved photos, prices, schedules, results, or review excerpts. Do not incentivize reviews or vary requests based on sentiment.

## Project-type prompts

Use only prompts that apply to the source record; omit unanswered prompts.

| Project type | Useful source-backed details to request |
|---|---|
| Kitchen | Homeowner goals, approved layout/design choices, verified scope, and documented result |
| Bathroom | Homeowner goals, approved selections, verified scope, and documented result |
| Whole home | Approved phases, coordination choices, verified scope, and documented result |
| Addition or ADU | Approved purpose, verified scope, and documented connection to the existing home |
| Home repair | Approved repair scope and documented completion; do not imply it is a service Miller offers until Erik confirms |

## Quarterly case-study outline

1. **Project context** — only the approved project type and broad location; omit identifying details.
2. **Homeowner goal** — use approved wording or a privacy-safe paraphrase approved by Erik.
3. **Scope and planning** — source-backed design and construction details.
4. **Challenges and decisions** — documented facts, with no unsupported causal claims.
5. **Outcome** — only verified results; omit quantified savings, schedule, or performance claims unless documented and approved.
6. **Media and proof** — exact approved assets, attribution, alt text, and usage rights.
7. **Next step** — owner-approved link and wording.
